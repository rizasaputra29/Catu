## Design Context

### Users
CATU is a personal/household finance PWA used primarily on mobile phones. Users are busy people tracking daily income, expenses, and wallets. They want to understand their money at a glance, add transactions quickly, and review monthly/yearly summaries without friction. The app is used in short bursts, often on the go, so clarity and calm are more important than visual excitement.

### Brand Personality
- **Calm**: no jarring neon, no aggressive shadows, no heavy outlines.
- **Capable**: data is organized, hierarchy is obvious, actions are clear.
- **Modern**: clean Apple/Linear/Vercel-inspired aesthetic with soft surfaces, rounded cards, and purposeful blue accents.

Three-word personality: calm, capable, modern.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Minimal, light-first SaaS**: white background, light gray cards (`bg-muted` / `#f8f9fa`), subtle borders (`border-border`), soft shadows (`shadow-sm`).
- **Single accent**: blue (`#0000ee` / `hsl(240 100% 46.7%)`) is the only primary action and brand color.
- **Semantic color**: emerald green is reserved exclusively for income, positive balances, and profit indicators; red is reserved for destructive actions and negative/expense signals. Never use green as a generic decorative accent.
- **Typography**: Onest (Google Font) for a friendly, modern sans-serif feel; clean sizes with `font-semibold` headings, no oversized hero type, no heavy tracking gimmicks.
- **Shape**: consistent `rounded-2xl` cards, `rounded-lg` / `rounded-full` buttons, no heavy black borders or offset shadows.
- **Motion**: short, subtle transitions (`fast 100ms`, `base 200ms`, `slow 300ms`), `ease-in-out`, no spring bounces, no large hover transforms.
- **Theme**: light mode only; dark mode is intentionally removed to keep the design focused and maintainable.

### Design Principles
1. **One blue accent**: use the primary blue for CTAs, active states, links, and focus rings. Green and red are reserved for semantic meaning.
2. **Borders are subtle**: use `border-border` (light gray) everywhere; no heavy black outlines, no 2px black strokes.
3. **Cards are calm**: white or light gray surfaces, `shadow-sm` for elevation, `rounded-2xl` corners.
4. **Type is restrained**: Onest, standard Tailwind scale, semibold headings, no custom oversized pixel values.
5. **Motion is quiet**: 100–300ms ease-in-out transitions, no aggressive hover scales, no spring physics, no offset shadows.
6. **Semantic color only**: green = income / positive; red = destructive / negative; blue = everything interactive.
7. **Swagger docs untouched**: API documentation pages keep their own styles; do not override them with the app design system.

### Anti-References
- Neo-brutalism: no heavy black borders, no lime/neo green backgrounds, no offset shadows.
- Cash App style: no vivid `#0000ee` on `#00e013` combinations, no display typography at 50px, no tight tracking.
- Dark mode complexity: no dual-theme tokens, no color-scheme media queries.
